# Digital Marketing Internship — Week 1

## Internship Name
Dawood tech next gen/Digital marketing

## Week Number
Week 1

## Objectives
Build a strong foundation in Digital Marketing by understanding core concepts, major marketing channels, the customer journey, and essential tools — while developing a professional online presence.

## Learning Topics
- Introduction to Digital Marketing (definition, traditional vs digital, importance, benefits, careers)
- Types of Digital Marketing: SEO, SMM, Content Marketing, Email Marketing, PPC, Affiliate, Influencer, Video
- Marketing Funnel & Customer Journey: Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Conversion → Retention → Loyalty
- Essential Tools: Canva, Google Trends, Google Analytics, Google Keyword Planner, Meta Business Suite, ChatGPT

## Practical Tasks
1. **Professional Marketing Profiles** — LinkedIn and Instagram optimized with bio, banner, skills, contact info
2. **Digital Marketing Research Report** — definitions, channels, 2026 trends, 5 company strategies
3. **Marketing Tools Exploration** — hands-on use of Canva, Google Trends, ChatGPT, Meta Business Suite
4. **Business Case Study** — analysis of one business's digital marketing strategy

## Tools Used
- Canva
- Google Trends
- ChatGPT
- Meta Business Suite
- LinkedIn / Instagram

## Key Learnings
- Digital marketing channels are either organic (SEO, Content), paid (PPC, Influencer), or direct (Email, Social)
- The marketing funnel maps every stage from discovery to loyal customer
- Tools like Canva and ChatGPT make content creation faster and more efficient
- Businesses succeed digitally through consistency, targeting, and data-driven decisions
